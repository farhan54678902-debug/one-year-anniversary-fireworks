রমজান ২০২৪ - মিষ্টি মায়া

ফেলে আসা সুন্দর মুহূর্ত

Goto Ramadan-er kotha mone pore? Ki shundor din gulo chilam amader! Protiti sehri-r opekkhaye thaka, iftarer pore tomar sathe kotha bola, shara diner klanti tomar ekti mishti hashite dure hoye jeto. Amra koto kachakachi chilam tokhon. Kintu aj eito amader dwitiyo Ramadan, kintu tumi amar theke onk dure. Somoy ebong poristhiti amader thik-i dure soralo, kintu amader mon-ke ki sora-te pereche? Protiti dowa-te ami shudhu tomy খুঁজি. Ei durotto ashole amader maya-ke aro bariye diyeche, protikhon ami shudhu tomar kotha-i bhabi.
